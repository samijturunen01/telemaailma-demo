## Flagged for verification (VERIFY)

1. **Founding year** – the company says 1977 (S5, P1); Fonecta lists a registration date of 5.12.1984 (likely the Ky's registration). Site uses 1977.
2. **"Suomen ensimmäinen Fronius Service Partner (tammikuu 2017)"** – from the 2020 archive (S7). The current site badge says *System Partner*. Confirm current partner level and whether the "first in Finland" claim may still be used.
3. **Motiva-certified installer** – sources from 2020–2021 (P1, P2, S7). Certification validity must be confirmed (Motiva certificates are time-limited).
4. **"1,5+ MWp asennettua vuoteen 2021 mennessä"** – from P2 (2021). An updated figure would be stronger.
5. **Canal Digital and ViaSat** – listed on S4, but both consumer brands merged into **Allente** in 2020. Recommend updating the wording.
6. **Samuli Pykäläinen / Tenho Pykäläinen** – named in P1 (2020). Confirm roles and permission to name them; no photos are used.
7. **Hybrid inverter / battery / hot-water storage and Finnish monitoring dashboards** – from S7 and P2; confirm these are still offered.
8. **"Latausasemat" product page** – linked from /tuotteet but returns "Sivua ei löydy". Not included; ask whether EV charging stations are sold.
9. **Phone/tablet out-of-warranty repairs** – P1 (2020). The demo only says "Kysy laitteesi huollosta" with the huolto@ address.
10. **Old SolarWorks direct contact** (Samuli Pykäläinen, +358 400 176 176, info@solarworks.fi, 2019–2020) – not used; confirm whether a direct solar contact should be published.
11. **Burrel "Black Week 24.–30.11.2025" banner** on the current homepage is a year out of date; the offer is not reproduced.
12. **IBC Solar** – the yield calculator is IBC Solar's; the 2019 "Preferred Premium Partner" badge (S7) was not used because it is dated.
13. **Valoe reseller** (S7, 2020) – not used; Valoe's status has changed since.
14. **Opening-hour exceptions** (public holidays) are unknown; the live "open now" badge uses regular hours only.
